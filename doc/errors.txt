Fixed errors:
=============

(at <location> refers to the original schematics)

SHEET 1

* At 1C3 the LS04 inverter is missing its designator. By inspecting the PCB
  it turns out to be U18.
* ~{MCS} is mislabeled as MCS.
* J1 pin 11 is not connected. On the PCB it has a solder pad next to the
  socket.

SHEET 2

* At 2A4, U15 LS02 is drawn as an AND with inverted inputs, not a NOR port.
  Logically, they are equivalent.

SHEET 3

* D9 and D10 have no value. Assembly instructions BOM lists 1N914.
* C65 at 3D3, has no value. Assembly Instructions, page 39, capacitor (C)
  is 150pF.
* Signal name 499.2 kHz does not match (3.932160*2)/16 = 491.52 kHz

SHEET 4

* U42 LS165 has the QH output pin mislabeled as QA.
* U15 LS02 at 4C2 is drawn as a NAND, but the LS02 is a NOR port. It is wired
  as an inverter, so logically it is identical (00->1, 11->0).

SHEET 5

* It seems there is a line missing between the positive side of C2 and C3.
* C1, C2, and C3 should be polarized.

Duplicate components:
=====================

R32     at 3D1 (1K) and 3B2 (1K). ---Renamed the one at 3B2 to R132.---
        Turned out the "R132" circuit connects to the _same_ R32 on the PCB.
R73     at 5B3 (68K) and 3A3 (470). Renamed the one near the LED to R173.
R74     at 2D1 (390) and 5B3 (510). Renamed the one at 2D1 to R174.
R86     at 3A4 (1K) and 3D1 (1K) and 5C3 (510).
        ---Renamed the 3D1 one to R186.--- Turned out "R186" connects to the
        same net on the PCB. There's only one, even though two are drawn.
        Renamed the 5C3 one to R286.
